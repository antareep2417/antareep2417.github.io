Physics-informed neural networks for interface problems

M.S. (Research) thesis · IIT Madras · 2021 – 2023 · with Dr. Chandrasekhar Annavarapu

Overview

Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) solve differential equations by training a network to satisfy the governing physics directly. Standard PINNs struggle when the material properties change abruptly across an interface, as in geological strata or around faults, because the solution is not smooth there.

This project developed PINN frameworks for heterogeneous physical systems with material interfaces. The aim is to model geometrically complex and highly heterogeneous subsurface systems that combine geological stratification with naturally occurring faults.

Methods

  • I-PINNs, a PINN framework for interface problems.
  • VI-PINNs, a variational formulation of interface PINNs, aimed at heterogeneous subsurface systems.
  • AdaI-PINNs, an adaptive variant developed with collaborators, aimed at making interface PINNs more efficient.
